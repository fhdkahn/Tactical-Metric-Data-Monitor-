How to use the designer

  1. Choose colors in the designer above.
  2. Copy the matching line of hex codes.
  3. Open Garmin Connect IQ → your watch → Tactical Metric / Data Monitor → Settings.
  4. Paste it into the matching custom-color field exactly as shown.

Why a value can show “--”

Some values depend on Garmin data becoming available. Weather and sun or moon event times require a phone connection and a valid weather location. Health fields can be unavailable if the device has not recorded enough recent data.

Seconds and the heart beat animation are intentionally limited when the display is inactive to protect battery life.

03 / Settings map

Quick setting reference

Settings appear in the Connect IQ app. Presets such as Design 1 set several related controls together, but every setting stays individually editable.

Design

Applies a curated starting combination of color theme, time font, face layout, weather row, and sun or moon row. Design 1 is the default for new installs.

Color theme

Default retains the tactical dark palette. Black on white swaps black and white elements while preserving intentional status colors such as red, amber, green, and blue.

Show time controls

Shows or hides the AM or PM indicator and battery control elements where the chosen time font layout supports them. Seconds remain context-aware.

Battery display

Choose bars, percentage, or estimated days. Percent values cap at 99 where the micro-font battery field cannot fit 100.

Top and bottom metrics

Choose running or cycling distance, weather or watch temperature, running or cycling VO₂ Max, and the four or three-column footer arrangement.

Data limitations

Metrics only show when Garmin exposes the matching data on the device. Weather and forecast fields depend on Garmin Connect and Bluetooth syncing.

Weekly streak icons

One day. Your longest activity.

Each day starts with its weekday letter. Once an activity is logged, the circle shows the activity that lasted the longest that day. If you log several activities, the one with the greatest duration determines the icon.

For example, a 45-minute ride and a 20-minute run show the cycling icon. An unknown activity type uses the Other / unknown activity icon.

Cycling

Shown when cycling is the longest logged activity of the day.

When no activity is logged

Today or a past day · no activity

A filled white background with the weekday letter means the day is today or has passed, with no activity logged yet. T means Tuesday in this example.

Future day

A patterned background with the weekday letter means the day is still ahead. It is neither today nor a past day. T means Tuesday in this example.

Main graph

Choose the style, metrics and history.

Seven styles are available: Dotted area + stress line, HR bars + stress line, HR line + stress line, HR lollipops + stress steps, Mirrored HR / stress bars, Layered HR / stress areas and Fine striped HR area + stress.

Either graph position can show Heart rate, Stress, Body Battery, Barometric pressure, Elevation, Temperature or Pulse Ox. The history choice is 1, 2 or 4 hours.
